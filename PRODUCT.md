# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who saw the "Outstanding" Tiny Desk AI meme on TikTok (October 2026) and want a version with their own face, usually to post a joke about themselves, their team, office or group chat. They arrive from search ("tiny desk ai", "outstanding tiny desk ai template") on a phone, mid-trend, and decide within seconds whether this site will make the clip for them.

## Product Purpose

tinydesktrend.com is an unofficial photo-to-video generator for one meme: upload one clear front-facing photo, get the 13-second lead-singer part of the "Outstanding" Tiny Desk clip recast with that face, vertical 9:16, 480p, with the song and a silent copy. Success is a visitor going from landing to a paid, downloaded clip, plus guide pages that answer the trend's search questions.

## Positioning

A single-meme specialist: one template done end to end (face check in the browser before upload, credits refunded on failure, song and silent versions trimmed in the browser) rather than a page inside a multi-template effects library.

## Operating Context

Visitors come mid-trend from TikTok and Google, mostly on phones. Account is email + password in a modal; credits are bought through Stripe Checkout; generation takes about 2 minutes and the page polls; videos are kept 7 days.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One photo, one role (lead singer), 13 s, 9:16, 480p; 100 credits per video.
- Packs: Starter $9.99 / 300 credits, Plus $19.99 / 700, Pro $49.99 / 1,800. One-time, no subscription.
- Failed generations refund credits automatically.
- Static pages generated by `build.py`; Worker `worker.js` with a strict CSP: scripts, styles and fonts from the site's own origin only (no inline styles, no hosted font CSS).
- Sold-out state exists when supply runs low.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Tiny Desk AI / Tiny Desk Trend, domain tinydesktrend.com.
- Must state it is an unofficial fan tool, not affiliated with NPR, NPR Music or Tiny Desk Concerts; no NPR or Tiny Desk logos or official assets.
- Visual reference chosen by the user (2026-10-29): intothememe.com's style.

## Evidence on Hand

- Sample output video `static/media/sample.mp4` with poster `static/media/sample.jpg` (13 s, 480p). The user chose to keep it.
- Seven guide pages and a FAQ with sourced facts (NPR, Rolling Stone, Wikipedia, WhoSampled, Hugging Face write-up).
- No testimonials, user counts, ratings or press. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. The generator is the page: the upload control is reachable in the first viewport on a phone.
2. State cost, length and format plainly before payment.
3. Consent and unofficial status are visible, not buried.
4. Never claim what the pipeline does not do (one role only, 480p, AI results vary).
