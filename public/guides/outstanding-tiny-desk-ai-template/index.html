Outstanding Tiny Desk AI Template: Shots, Timing and Who Goes Where

Updated 2026-10-28

The "template" everyone recasts is the vertical cut of the last ~19 seconds of Charlie Wilson's 2023 Tiny Desk performance of "Outstanding". Specs below come from a frame-by-frame breakdown.

Format

  • Vertical 9:16, 720×1280
  • 24 fps
  • 19.06 seconds
  • Five shots, with a hard cut at exactly 13.0 seconds

The five shots

ShotTime (s)What's in it
10–1.375Lead singer's opening line, side-on
21.375–6.708Band: keys in front, two singers behind
36.708–13.0The big laugh close-up of the lead
413.0–16.375Sax solo
516.375–19.06The band again

Roles you can recast

  • Lead singer: Shots 1 and 3, including the big laugh close-up. This is the role almost every version recasts.
  • Keys player: Front and centre in the band shots (2 and 5). Easy to mistake for the lead at first glance.
  • Two backing singers: Behind the keys in the band shots.
  • Sax player: Gets the solo in shot 4, right after the 13-second cut, so it often goes to the surprise cast member.

Tips

  • Use one person per role. Putting the same face in two roles made swaps fail in published tests.
  • Use the vertical cut, not the widescreen original, so the framing matches.
  • Keep the song timing intact so the audio lines up.
