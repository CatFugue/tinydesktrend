What Is the Song in the Tiny Desk AI Video?

Updated 2026-10-28

Short answer: "Outstanding" by The Gap Band, from the 1982 album The Gap Band IV. The meme uses Charlie Wilson's live version, the closing song of his NPR Tiny Desk concert published June 5, 2023.

Who is Charlie Wilson?

Charlie Wilson was the lead singer of The Gap Band, which he formed with his brothers Robert and Ronnie; the group's hits ran from the 1970s through the '80s. He later became a frequent hip-hop collaborator, nicknamed "Uncle Charlie" by Snoop Dogg. (NPR)

  2. Computer Love
  3. There Goes My Baby
  4. I'm Blessed
  5. Yearning For Your Love
  6. Sweet Yamz
  7. Outstanding

Source: NPR, Rolling Stone.
